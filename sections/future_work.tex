\section{Future Work and Improvements}

The prototype demonstrates proof of concept, but substantial opportunities exist for enhanced robustness and practical utility.

\subsection{Additional Data Sources}
The current implementation utilizes Google Trends, app store reviews, and basic metadata. Enhanced data richness would improve prediction accuracy. Future work could incorporate social media discussions on platforms such as Twitter, Reddit, and LinkedIn where users discuss productivity tools and workflow challenges. Community forum activity—including official Notion community boards and Product Hunt threads where early adopters share unfiltered feedback—represents another valuable source. Competitor monitoring systems tracking rival product feature launches and resulting shifts in public interest would provide additional context.

\subsection{Advanced Modeling Approaches}
The statistical methods employed have decades of validation and remain robust. However, contemporary techniques could improve accuracy further. Neural network architectures such as LSTMs or Transformers demonstrate strength in learning complex temporal patterns. Causal inference frameworks could help distinguish genuine causal relationships from mere correlation. Streaming prediction systems could update forecasts continuously rather than waiting for batch processing cycles.

\subsection{Production Deployment}
Approaches effective in research contexts may not survive contact with real users without modification. Serious deployment would require infrastructure capable of handling traffic spikes—appropriate caching, load distribution, and database optimization. Automated pipelines would be needed to detect model drift from reality and trigger retraining before predictions become stale. Enterprise-grade security features including role-based permissions and detailed audit trails would be essential.

\subsection{Dashboard Enhancement}
The current interface provides basic functionality, but product managers could benefit from additional capabilities. Custom alert systems could notify teams when key metrics cross significant thresholds. Scenario planning tools could enable "what-if" analysis showing how different choices might affect outcomes. Direct integration with project management platforms such as Jira or Linear could enable insights to flow directly into sprint planning workflows.

\subsection{Generalization Beyond Notion}
This research examined only one product in depth. To establish framework reliability, future work should apply the same analysis to multiple SaaS products to assess pattern consistency. Cases where predictions can be compared against actual adoption metrics would enable measurement of real accuracy. User studies with actual product managers would assess whether the tool genuinely improves decision-making quality.
