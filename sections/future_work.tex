\section{Future Work and Improvements}

The prototype already shows evidence of the concept, but there is a lot of room on stronger robustness and usability.

Other sources of data are also available.
The existing application uses Google Trends, the reviews of the apps, and simple metadata. Richness of data would be enhanced which would increase the accuracy of prediction. It may be used in the future in the form of social media discussions in which users share their thoughts on different productivity tools and workflow issues on platforms like Twitter, Reddit, and LinkedIn. Community activity: Community forum: Community boards on official Notion, Product hunt threads where early adopters post raw feedback are also a source of value. The rival product feature launches and subsequent changes in the general interest would offer more context through the competitor monitoring systems tracking their launches.

The sophisticated model making techniques are discussed.
The statistical procedures used have been over fifty years old and still sound. The modern methods, however, may increase accuracy. LSTM neural network architectures or Transformer neural networks have been shown to be strong when it comes to learning intricate time series. Inferential causal models may be useful in differentiating real causal laws and correlation laws. The predictions streaming systems may also be used to update predictions without following a batch processing cycle.

\subsection{Production Deployment}
The strategies that can be successfully applied in a research setting might not withstand the exposure to the actual users. There would be a need of appropriate infrastructures required to support spurring up of traffic such as proper caching, load balancing, and database optimization. There would be a necessity of automated pipelines to tell when a given model drifts off reality and causes retraining before predictions become stale. Security features such as role-based permissions and advanced audit trails would be necessary on an enterprise-level.

\subsection{Dashboard Enhancement}
The existing interface is simple in nature and product managers might need more features. Individual alert systems can be used to make teams aware when critical metrics pass large values. Scenario planning tools might allow the analysis of what-if analysis of how various decisions would impact the results. Build-in connectivity with project management systems like Jira or Linear would possibly allow insights to be pushed right into the sprint planning processes.

\subsection{Generalization Beyond Notion}
This means that certain concepts can be extended to other concepts, in general. This study has focused on one product in detail. In order to define the framework reliability, the repeatability of patterns should be employed to various SaaS products in the future. The cases that could be compared with the actual adoption metrics on the predictions would allow the measurement of actual accuracy. The testability of the tool to improve the quality of the decision-making process would be measured by user studies involving real product managers.
